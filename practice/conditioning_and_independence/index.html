Definitions
Conditional probability
P(A | B) describes the share of the probability in B that also lies in A, when P(B) is positive.
Joint probability
P(A and B) describes both events occurring, without restricting the sample space first.
Independence
Events are independent when their joint probability equals the product of their marginal probabilities. Random variables are independent when every joint probability factors this way.
