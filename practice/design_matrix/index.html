Index and shape guide

Row i is observation i. The four design columns, in order, are 1, x, g, and xg. Group A has g = 0 and group B has g = 1. The product xg lets the groups have different slopes.

In the formulas, Xij means X[i, j]; βj means beta[j]. A residual is observed y minus predicted y. Coefficients are supplied, not fitted in this activity.
