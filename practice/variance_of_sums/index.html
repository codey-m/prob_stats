Definitions
Covariance
Covariance measures joint variation. Positive covariance raises the variance of a sum; negative covariance lowers it.
Variance of a sum
For variables with finite variances, add their variances and twice their covariance.
Scaling a random variable
Multiplying a random variable by c multiplies its variance by c².
Uncorrelated and independent
Independent variables with finite variances have zero covariance. Zero covariance alone does not establish independence.
