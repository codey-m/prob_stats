Limits as something grows

Problem sets ask what happens to a quantity as a sample size grows, or whether it settles at a value at all. This page checks one move: find the term that grows fastest, and see what it leaves behind.

The rules you need

  • A fixed number divided by a growing quantity shrinks to 0: 3/n → 0 and 1/n² → 0.
  • For a ratio, divide every term on top and bottom by the fastest-growing power. That leaves the ratio of the leading coefficients when both grow at the same rate.
  • If the top grows faster, the ratio grows without bound. If the bottom grows faster, the ratio goes to 0.
  • An exponential en grows faster than any power of n.
