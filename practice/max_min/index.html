Finding a maximum or minimum

Estimation problems ask for the parameter value that makes a likelihood or posterior as large as possible. This page checks the move: take logs if the function is a product, differentiate, set the derivative to zero, then check that you found a maximum inside the allowed range.

The rules you need

  • ln is increasing, so a positive function and its log have their largest value at the same point. Logs turn products into sums: ln(ab) = ln a + ln b and ln(eu) = u.
  • d/dθ ln θ = 1/θ, and d/dθ θn = n θn−1.
  • At an interior maximum the derivative is zero. Zero alone is not enough: it must change from positive to negative there.
  • Keep only solutions in the allowed range, and compare with the ends of the range. The largest value can sit at an end.
