Definitions
Likelihood
For fixed observations, the joint probability mass or density evaluated at those observations, considered as a function of the candidate parameter. It need not sum or integrate to 1 over parameter values.
Log-likelihood
The logarithm of the likelihood. Maximizing it gives the same parameter ranking as maximizing a positive likelihood.
Posterior probability
Probability assigned to parameter values after observing data, obtained by combining a prior with the likelihood and normalizing.
Gaussian likelihood and squared deviations
For independent normal observations with a common unknown mean μ and fixed known SD σ, log-likelihood is C − Σ(yᵢ − μ)²/(2σ²). Here C is independent of μ.
