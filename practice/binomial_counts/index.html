Definitions
Independent trials
Trials whose results do not change each other’s probabilities. The probability of a particular sequence of results is the product of the probabilities of its individual results.
Factorial
n! = n × (n − 1) × … × 2 × 1, the number of orderings of n distinct items. By convention, 0! = 1.
Choosing k of n positions
The number of ways to choose k positions from n when order does not matter, read “n choose k”: n!/(k!(n − k)!). Choosing the k success positions also fixes the n − k failure positions.
Disjoint events
Events that cannot occur together. The probability that one of them occurs is the sum of their probabilities.
