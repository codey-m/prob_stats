Definitions
Partition
Mutually exclusive groups that together cover all possible outcomes.
Total probability
Weight each within-group event probability by the probability of that group, then add across the partition.
Total expectation
Weight each conditional mean by the probability of that group, then add across the partition.
Group weights
The population probabilities of the groups. They are not automatically equal and need not equal the within-group event probabilities.
