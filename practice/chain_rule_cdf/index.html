The chain rule

To find the density of a transformed variable, you differentiate a CDF whose argument is itself a function. This page checks one move: differentiate the outer function, evaluate it at the inner function, and multiply by the inner function's derivative.

About 3 minutes if you remember it. About 10 if you use the reminder.

The rules you need

  • Chain rule: d/dy G(h(y)) = G′(h(y)) · h′(y). The outer derivative is evaluated at the inner function.
  • d/du eu = eu, so d/du e−u = −e−u. A constant has derivative 0.
  • d/dy yn = n yn−1.
  • For a CDF F with density f: d/dy F(h(y)) = f(h(y)) · h′(y).
