Definitions
Posterior distribution
The conditional distribution of the unknown quantity after observing the data. It retains probabilities for all its possible values.
MAP estimate
A value with the largest posterior probability. For a discrete parameter it maximizes the probability of an exactly correct guess.
Posterior mean
The probability-weighted mean under the posterior. It minimizes posterior expected squared error when estimates may be real numbers.
Loss function
A rule assigning a cost to an estimate and a true value. The preferred estimate depends on this rule.
