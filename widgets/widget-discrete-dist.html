Discrete distributions: PMF and CDF

Pick a distribution and its parameters. The top panel is the probability mass function. The bottom panel is the cumulative distribution function F(k) = P(X ≤ k), the probability that the variable comes out at or below k; it steps up by the PMF value at each k and climbs from 0 to 1. The dashed line marks the mean and the bar on the axis marks one standard deviation each way.
